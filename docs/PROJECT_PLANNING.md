## 商业假设 / Commercial assumptions

以下是原演示的验证假设，不是正在销售的报价或已取得的收益。
These are demonstration assumptions, not an active sales offer or measured benefits.

| 项目 / Item | 原假设 / Original assumption |
| --- | --- |
| 目标客户 / Target | 5–20 艘船的船东及管理公司 / Owners and managers with 5–20 ships |
| 年费及接入 / Fee and setup | €4,800/year for ≤5 ships + €600 first-year onboarding |
| 单客变动成本 / Variable cost | €1,600 = 16h QC × €60 + 8h support × €35 + €360 cloud/data |
| 单客贡献 / Contribution | €3,200; gross margin 66.7% |
| 固定成本及经营平衡 / Fixed costs and break-even | €180,000; 57 full-year equivalent clients; about 60 with the assumed 15% churn replacement expense |
| 质控工时敏感性 / QC sensitivity | 32h: margin 46.7%, about 81 clients |
| 客户价值 / Client value | 120h × €40 + €2,000 cancellable advice − €4,800 − €600 = €1,400 in year 1; −€600 without advice savings |

工时节省不一定兑现为现金；顾问费不可重复计益。
Time savings need not become cash savings; avoid double counting adviser fees.

## 市场验证、人的负担与 ESG / Validation, workload and ESG

- 计划 12 家访谈、3 家付费试点；尚未取得实测结果。与软件、顾问和表格按同口径比较，并验证付费意愿。
- 拟继续条件：至少 2 家愿按报价续订，实测净价值覆盖费用，无未解决重大计算差异。
- 区分使用者、采购方、法定责任方和合同偿付方；记录船员、岸基及客户的数据准备、更正、培训工时，保留质疑与更正渠道。
- E：云、办公和差旅绝对量及每报告强度，ESG 负责人每月记录；客户结果须区分预算与实际证据，由分析负责人逐报告核对。
- S：新增劳动按角色记录，由人员负责人逐试点核对。
- G：版本、复核、重大差异、佣金和负面结果由治理负责人逐次记录。基准、目标、实测均尚未建立。

Plan 12 interviews and 3 paid pilots; no measured results yet. Compare against software, advisers and spreadsheets. Proposed continuation criteria: two renewals at the quoted price, measured net value exceeding fees, and no unresolved material calculation differences. Record added crew/shore/client workload and correction routes. Monthly ESG work covers cloud, office and travel; report-level work distinguishes estimated from observed customer outcomes. Track workload per pilot and governance evidence per change. Baselines, targets and measurements remain unestablished.

## 风险与治理 / Risk and governance

| 风险 / Risk | 控制与剩余问题 / Controls and remaining issues |
| --- | --- |
| 规则变化 / Rule changes | 官方来源和版本记录；已有自填双人复核记录，非身份认证或专家批准，法律解释仍需复核 / Official sources and revisions; self-entered second-person records are not authenticated expert approval, and legal interpretation needs review |
| 数据与模型 / Data and model | 缺失气体不填零，只显示 CO₂ 部分；原始数据、覆盖范围及系数仍待核实 / Preserve missing gases, show CO₂ subtotals; source data, scope and factors need confirmation |
| AI 建议 / AI advice | 已有需同意及课堂码的云端AI解释；不实时检索法规，重要建议仍需专业复核 / Consented cloud AI with a classroom code is available; no live legal retrieval, and material advice requires professional review |
| 网络与隐私 / Cyber and privacy | 已部署独立AI代理；客户数据仍为本机工作区，无客户云端账户或船载连接，正式隔离、加密、恢复和责任条款待验证 / A separate AI proxy is deployed; customer records remain local, with no cloud workspace or shipboard connection. Production isolation, encryption, recovery and liability terms are unverified |
| 漂绿与责任 / Greenwashing and liability | 财务与 WtW 模型分列，预算不冒充核验；仍有赔偿与声誉风险 / Separate cost and WtW estimates, do not equate budgets with verification; liability and reputation risks remain |
| 获客、续订与交付 / Acquisition, retention, delivery | 访谈和试点为计划，记录实际支持与质控工时；客户不足、超支和资金缺口待验证 / Interviews and pilots planned; measure support/QC effort; customer, cost and funding risks remain |

原页面的员工奖励独立性等陈述属于拟议治理政策，当前没有运营制度或外部鉴证可支持将其作为已落实承诺。未来真实推荐若涉及佣金，应向客户披露。
Original compensation-independence statements are proposed governance policies, not assured operational controls. Disclose referral commissions to customers if recommendations are offered in future.
