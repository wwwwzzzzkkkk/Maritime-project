## 中文

- 顶栏“分析与报告”：显式本机保存／恢复、可恢复工作区文件、可打印独立 HTML 决策报告。包含当前预算、六类方案、条件、公式、台账来源、规划情景及必要限制。恢复按输入重算，旧快照继续作为只读证据。
- “方案比较”新增折叠的统一 CSV 台账编辑／导入与初步范围检查。原始燃油行同时推导能源和 CO₂，再按范围加权。应用后对应能源、强度和距离只读；可回编辑区修改。资料来源保留，草稿变更清空旧预览，恢复能还原带逗号／引号的来源。
- 本版载入范围为普通 2026 年、GT>5000、有支持的 EU 能源与航行距离的货／客船。ETS/FuelEU 门槛差别、UK 7 月起始、挪威／冰岛及特殊范围转复核。不是完整法律适用或认证核算，缺失气体不补零。
- 具名规划情景保存价格、FuelEU 目标和组池条件，连同保存时船舶和数据比较 EUR／GBP。保存不自动改变预算，明确载入才应用；不引入全球假设收费或概率。
- 新增 AI 客户端和本机后端，按用户选择默认未连接。密钥只在服务端；同意后发送摘要，附固定来源，回答变化后标记过期。未部署公网服务，也未进行真实模型调用；provider 与浏览器响应检查使用模拟。
- 保留原玻璃、动态效果、五项快速输入、EUR/GBP 分列及六项基准金额。五个受保护文件逐字节不变，引擎与既有搜索算法未改。用户确认不代表核验、专家批准或法律意见。
- 修正工作区校验以容纳既有组池请求／供给的 10000 tCB 范围，避免保存合法大于 400 的请求失败；默认不变。

## English

- Analysis & Reports provides explicit local saves/restores, restorable workspaces and a standalone printable HTML report with six option families, conditions, formulas, data sources and limitations. Restore recomputes; old snapshots remain read-only evidence.
- A collapsed unified CSV editor derives scoped energy and CO₂ from common fuel rows. Derived inputs are read-only while active. Draft edits clear previews, and restoration rebuilds the editor with correct quoting.
- Ordinary supported 2026 cargo/passenger scope only: GT>5000 with supported EU energy/distance for this estimator. Threshold differences, UK commencement and review cases are explicit. Missing gases are not zero; this is not certified/legal-scope verification.
- Named explicit planning conditions retain their saved vessel/data, compare EUR/GBP separately, and change the budget only when loaded. No invented global charge or probability.
- AI UI and loopback backend are implemented but disconnected by default, as chosen by the user. Server-side keys, consented summaries, bundled citations and stale-answer warnings are provided. No hosted AI service or genuine model-call verification; responses were mocked in tests.
- Preserve original glass/motion, five quick inputs, all six benchmarks, protected sources and regulatory/data limitations. Engine/search functions remain unchanged.
